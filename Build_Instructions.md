**Step 1: Assemble the steering system**

  1. On the edges of the long ackermann steering rod, attach the left and right ackermann tabs with two 1.00 inch VEX screws and lock nuts, positioned such that the tabs are curved inward. Tighten until the tabs don’t shake, but don’t overtighten. The tabs should spin freely.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7dfde89d-42e4-477d-ae5a-bc98042036e6" /> <img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/edafd73c-09ec-4265-b3f6-a07c77f9323e" />

  2. Attach the short ackermann steering rod on the same plane as the long ackermann steering rod, with the same type of two screws and nuts. Same tightening rules.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/63fa0daf-5815-4e12-af13-12fb914864b6" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2efe202a-81cc-470d-bd47-6e4ccf3e4571" />

  3. Mount the long ackermann steering rod onto the front of the chassis using two 1.25 inch VEX screws and lock nuts, positioned such that the short ackermann rod is facing the back of the chassis and the ackermann tabs are on a lower plane than the long and short ackermann rods.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/437c5149-a828-49fb-be8b-d68950d59a8d" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eaf86c28-72a5-43f2-9b82-8f0a475f7820" />

  4. Attach the two steering LEGO Spike Prime wheels onto the outsides of the ackermann tabs using two 1.00 inch LEGO axles with stoppers, and two axle spacers.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1da3bfed-8d3b-441c-b063-c70a297fcb81" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/568d2a6b-001d-43d2-a9fb-a85c67d47b2b" />

  5. Mount the camera and servo mount onto the fixed long ackermann rod using 1.25 inch VEX screws and lock nuts.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0081e1d9-8c8b-4c8c-b6f2-0aeee70b446b" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e003887e-6d8f-421e-bd64-577b9c5e4ff4" />

  6. Attach the servo box mount using two 2.00 inch VEX screws and lock nuts, angled such that the servo hole is positioned over the remaining hole in the long ackermann rod. Remember to keep space under the box for the hexagonal ackermann rod.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3852aca9-59f5-403a-8c73-009d6c414617" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/34b306f7-406b-43a2-a9af-542900d5345b" />

  7. Fit a 1.85 inch long LEGO axle into the servo-axle coupling. Thread the axle down through the servo box, through the hexagonal ackermann rod, and through the aforementioned hole. Use a LEGO technic attach piece to attach the other side of the hexagonal ackermann rod to the hole in the middle of the short ackermann rod.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e173d918-9f07-44b7-8cbe-7795e4c79486" />

Screw in the MG90S servo into the servo box using a ______ M2 screw, with the servo fitted into the servo-axle coupling (MAKE SURE: before you fit the servo, the steering is straight, and the servo angle is 90 degrees. **Step 2: Assemble the drivetrain and chassis body**

  1. Push a 1.575 inch LEGO axle through a LEGO Spike Prime wheel and an axle spacer as shown in the image, then push the axle through the axle hole until the tip of the axle is flush with the wheel. Add an axle spacer on the outside of the chassis. Do the same on the other side.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e78d057-d90a-4092-b0f3-fb4a3939f7e8" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/66e70eee-e399-48c7-8c58-5d8c005133b3" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5f3e04fe-2b25-4a7a-918d-50ba22b32703" />

  2. Push the two differential-axle coupling pieces into the differential sockets such that the ridges on the coupling slide into the valleys on the differential.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/be5a0ecf-c729-4c07-9f7c-ca2f988dcfec" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ccf8fdfc-66d3-4446-a490-6f965777f085" />

  3. Place the differential into its designated position, with the metal gear on the right side. Then, push the axles into the differential-axle couplings. Might take a little back-and-forth but should slide right in once the right angle is found.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d3bbaeee-4a31-4300-b44a-af384547ecc2" />

  4. Slide the bearing onto the shaft of the pinion and connect the motor-differential coupling. This has a specific alignment but twisting and pushing it should eventually make it fall into place.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bda9d359-c0c4-4a88-91ec-dd78580c78b7" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/234a0c5a-4cce-412f-b187-083607a9ac6c" />

  5. Place the motor in its place with the wires facing upwards. Attach the pinion with the coupling to the motor. This is a tight fit, so it will take some finagling. Ensure the bearing is in its designated semicircle cut and the motor screw hole aligns with the hole in the chassis motor mount. Then screw the motor in using an M2 screw.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c6149c79-34cc-4adf-8620-10b1ddfc989a" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b41a14df-2021-4222-8823-bf58daa9123f" />

  6. Position the differential housing above the differential such that the bearing clamps are positioned over the three bearings and the holes in the corners go through the chassis. Use four 1.50 inch VEX screws with lock nuts to fasten the housing into place.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0f7958b-c7d3-408e-af18-463ece84960f" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fcaf02b-a746-4211-ad61-96b47cbb7a39" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1418760d-5917-455e-91d8-8eb133a38aaa" />

  7. In the four holes around the motor, screw in four 1.00 inch standoffs using 0.375 inch VEX screws. Place the middle plate onto the standoffs and screw it in using four more 0.375 inch VEX screws.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d1456d17-2840-4951-9d69-151a2823742f" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e148d971-abe3-4c83-9df1-edd3733b0260" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2debc951-75ca-4fcf-9c82-a9e0657030e3" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aed53615-311e-4b9e-a0b1-bd48fc894bba" />

**Mount Electronics**

  1. Using two small zip ties, tighten the arduino nano with its shield onto the side of the middle plate that has holes. 

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b7296dfe-ab0c-4b01-a913-81804631af03" />

  2. Screw in a 1.00 inch and 0.75 inch standoff on the bottom left and top right holes on the edge of the area around the rear differential, with the two 1.50 inch VEX screw coming up from the bottom of the chassis.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/db14357a-10a3-41c7-8a76-4682cfb2f84c" />

  3. Place the Raspberry Pi 5 on the Pi plate. Attach it with four small zip ties to the plate.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3725b29f-adbe-4aae-9c67-614645416a7f" />
<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/40f1054e-f539-4e24-9401-461a17c28eb3" />


  4. Screw in the Pi plate using two 0.375 inch VEX screws screwed into the standoffs. Make sure the USB-A ports are facing the right side of the robot, the same side as the differential gear.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/426f33c5-a663-4a85-be39-389af0972a67" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cccaebbd-4de6-4b4d-b22e-5d0b52fdc2f5" />

  5. Connect the USB-A port on the step-down buck converter to the USB-C port on the Raspberry Pi 5. Also connect the USB-C port on the Arduino Nano to the bottom right port of the Raspberry Pi 5’s USB-A ports.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1605f1d2-4f31-490d-82cf-ab641c78df94" />

  6. Wire the motor driver and Arduino Nano using the wiring diagram provided above in 2.5.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c68ef40d-37a8-43e5-a220-4900b9cbf28a" />
<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f79ecc80-e3dc-4397-8ddc-4c5525e5be84" />

  7. With the heatsink facing downwards, zip tie the motor driver onto the chassis using one on the bottom left hole, intersecting with the hole near it, and the top right hole of the motor driver, intersecting with the hole on the Pi plate.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a27a4b5e-1e18-4210-817c-7f7458a9aba6" />

  8. Wire the rest of the Raspberry Pi 5 connections using the aforementioned wiring diagram. Plug the 5V power wire from the step-down buck converter into the Arduino Nano.

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/232ee832-59bb-426c-9ac7-601eaa11ee67" />

  9. Push the toggle switch into the right most hole on the Control Panel and solder it to the circuit using the wiring diagram, so that the switch controls ALL power going to the robot. 

<img width="130.2" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6a3954c2-4d3d-4b14-a9ba-553fa8754474" />

  10. Screw in the BNO055 IMU using two M2 screws on the top two holes, positioned in the middle of the Control Panel. Use zip ties to attach the OLED screen on the left side of the Control Panel, tied through the top two holes. 

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0e0a89e-c227-4fee-93d5-80d7a784ef4d" />

  11. Screw in the start button into the remaining spot on the Control Panel using an M3 screw. Wire it according to the wiring diagram.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a93a67b7-2c5d-49aa-9b7e-d67f0a9d51af" />

  12. Slide the last 3D part, the battery holder, into place as shown in the image below. Make sure the holder is not all the way down because it may rub against the steering wheels. 

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8f305e05-71e8-49ce-b2db-0cc9ea6dbb24" />

13. Place the 12.6V battery into its spot and connect the power wire from the 12V side of the step-down buck converter to the corresponding wire on the battery.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6c203cb2-570a-4b5b-98fd-c04f9e4622bf" />

14. Gently pull up the CSI tab and slide in the Camera wire such that the dark part is facing the tab, then push the tab down to hold the wire in place. Slide the camera upside down into its place on the top front of the Camera mount, with the wire coming out of the top.

<img width="231.8" height="173.8" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/96c9bf42-6562-4b09-8d71-a2f28058c973" />
